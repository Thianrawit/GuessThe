## Requirements Addressed
1. **Two-Stage Question Flow (Snippet Playing -> Answering Countdown)**:
   - Separated snippet duration (`snippetDuration`) from answer timer (`guessDuration`).
   - During snippet playback (Stage 1), choice buttons are strictly **DISABLED** (`opacity-60`, `cursor-not-allowed`) and cannot be clicked. Status indicator informs players to wait for snippet completion.
   - Once snippet completes (Stage 2), choice buttons become **ENABLED**, and the Answer Timer Bar starts counting down from 100% to 0%.
   - When <= 3s remains in the answer timer, Urgency Effects activate (flashing neon red-orange bar, heartbeat scale pulse, 🚨 icon, red timer text).
2. **Speed-Based Scoring System**:
   - For correct answers: Points = Base Points - Time Deduction.
   - Deduction rule: For every 500ms (0.5s) elapsed, deduct 0.5 points (`Math.floor(timeUsedMs / 500) * 0.5`).
   - Minimum score for correct answers is clamped at 0.5pt.
   - Incorrect answers or timeouts (0s) yield 0 points.
   - Formatted scores display integers cleanly (e.g., 10) or with one decimal place (e.g., 9.5).
3. **Multiplayer Leaderboard Crowns vs Solo Mode**:
   - Ranked from highest score to lowest score.
   - Multiplayer mode:
     - Rank 1: Gold Crown (`👑` with amber glow & bounce).
     - Rank 2: Silver Crown (`👑` with silver monochrome styling).
     - Rank 3: Bronze Crown (`👑` with sepia/bronze styling).
     - Rank 4+: Rank index (`#4`, `#5`, etc.).
   - Solo mode: Crowns are completely hidden in front of the player name.
4. **Zero Thumbnail Bleed in Audio Mode**:
   - Ensured `#yt-player-container` has `opacity-0` during audio guessing.
   - Made `#media-curtain` 100% pitch solid black (`bg-[#0a0a14] z-20`) without any transparency or backdrop blur leak.
   - Unveils video smoothly at full opacity during the reveal phase.
